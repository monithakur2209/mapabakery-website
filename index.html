Security & Trust

We take your privacy and payment security seriously so you can order with confidence.

How we keep your data safe

  • All orders are processed over secure, encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Payments are handled by PayPal — we never store card details.
  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing on our servers.
  • We only collect the minimum information needed to fulfil your order.
