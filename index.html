Cake Sizes & Serving Guide

Choose the perfect size for your celebration. These are our standard cake sizes and approximate servings:

  • 6-inch — serves 10–12 people
  • 8-inch — serves 20–24 people
  • 10-inch — serves 30–40 people
  • 12-inch — serves 50–60 people

Flavours & Options

  • Egg or Eggless options available
  • Fresh cream or buttercream
  • Chocolate, Vanilla, Butterscotch
  • Custom designs available (send reference images)

Ordering Tips

  • For photo cakes or custom designs, please order at least 1 week in advance
  • All cakes are freshly prepared on the day of pickup/delivery
  • For allergies, mention details in the notes section

Security & Trust

We take your privacy and security seriously so you can order with confidence.

How we keep your data safe

  • All orders are processed over secure, encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Payments are handled during pick-up/delivery — we never store card details.
  • We only collect the minimum information needed to fulfil your order.
  • We never share your information with third parties.
